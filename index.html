          💌 Our Story

          About TravelMate

          Made for dreamers, slow-travellers, and scrapbook lovers everywhere.

          Why We Made TravelMate 🧸

          Planning a trip shouldn't feel like filling out a dry corporate spreadsheet. We believe holidays should begin the moment you dream them up—full of colors, washi tapes, polaroids, and whimsical notes.

          TravelMate brings back the tactile joy of paper scrapbooking into a smooth, responsive, friendly digital companion. No annoying ads, no bloated setups—just your travel joy preserved like sweet pressed flowers.

          “To travel is to live, to laugh, and to collect lovely moments.”

          The Scrapbook Philosophy 🌿

          • 🎨
            Pastel & Calm: Warm creams and soothing candy tones that inspire gentle adventure.
          • 🎒
            All In One Place: Itinerary, packing lists, budget balances, and destination postcards.
          • ✨
            Interactive & Offline: Instant updates right in your browser with zero sign-up friction.
          • 🌸
            Made With Love: Crafted with pure HTML, CSS and JS for snappy performance.
